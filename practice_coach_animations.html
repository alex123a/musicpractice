Animation Guidelines for "More Expressive"

Why these animations work for Practice Coach:

  • Durations: 300ms–800ms — Fast enough to feel responsive, slow enough to feel thoughtful. Never snappy or instant.
  • Easing curves: Mix of ease-out, cubic-bezier with bounce. Not linear. Matches how encouragement feels — starts energetic, settles calmly.
  • Celebration moments pulse. Success animations (pulse ring, bounce) feel expressive and affirming because they reward effort.
  • Subtle loops (1.2s–2s) for live states. Recording pulse, focus glow keep user engaged without demanding attention.
  • Stagger reveals organize information. Lists appearing one-by-one (100ms delays) feel thoughtful, not overwhelming.
  • 3D transforms (flip, rotate) for special moments. Used sparingly, they feel elegant and memorable — not overused.
  • Always respond to interaction. Every button press, scroll, navigation gets tactile feedback. Makes app feel alive.
  • Entrance animations set tone. New questions/focus options slide in with bounce — says "I'm excited to help you practice."
Accessibility: All animations respect prefers-reduced-motion. Users who disable animations still get instant, non-animated versions of all interactions.

Motion Budget: The app uses ~9 different animation types across different screens. This variety keeps the experience fresh without feeling chaotic. Each animation has a purpose — never decorative, always purposeful.
