Choose a practice strategy:
  • Drone practice

    Play with a drone note to anchor pitch

    Start with the tonic drone, then practice each problematic interval separately. Stop on each note and compare to the drone before moving forward.
  • Tempo reduction

    Start at 40% speed, increase gradually

    Use your metronome and reduce tempo until you can play every note cleanly. Increase by 5 BPM increments only when you can play three times without error.
  • Vocal modeling

    Sing the passage first to internalize pitch

    Sing the passage on pitch names or syllables before playing. This builds your inner aural image and makes physical execution easier.
  • Interval exaggeration

    Practice with exaggerated intervals

    Overshoot the target note slightly, then correct. This builds physical awareness of where correct pitch lives in your body and on the instrument.
  • Prepare target note

    Secure the note before the shift

    Stop before the problematic note. Place the finger, check intonation, then play. Build muscle memory for the correct position before adding flow.
