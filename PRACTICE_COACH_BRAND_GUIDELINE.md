## Brand Identity

### Name
**Practice Coach** — Your supportive guide for independent, diagnostic practice

**Tagline:**  
*"Practice smarter, not harder"*

**Secondary messaging:**
- "Your diagnostic practice partner"
- "Professional practice methodology, anytime"
- "Focus on what matters most"

### Selected Direction
**Terracotta-Warm** — Warm, human, friendly. Teal as calm anchor. Approachable feel.

This direction was chosen to emphasize the **supportive coaching relationship** while maintaining **calm clarity** through the teal anchor. Warm and welcoming, never clinical or elite.

---

## Brand Personality

### Core Values
- **Supportive** — Like a thoughtful teacher who believes in you
- **Empowering** — Builds independence and critical thinking
- **Clear-headed** — Cuts through confusion, focuses attention
- **Approachable** — Warm, human, never elite or clinical
- **Systematic** — Methodology you can trust and repeat

### Tone of Voice
- **Friendly but focused** — conversational, not stilted
- **Encouraging without being patronizing** — respect your effort and intelligence
- **Clear and direct** — eliminate jargon, get to the point
- **Active and present** — "Let's work through this" not "You should..."

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Play the passage a few more times. Give yourself a chance to settle in."
- ❌ "Repeat the problematic section 5 times."
- ✅ "What exactly is the problem? Listen carefully and try to exaggerate the error."
- ❌ "Identify your mistake."

## Typography

### Font Selections
**Goal:** Warm, friendly, and approachable. Professional but human. Never cold or corporate.

#### Display Font (Headlines, App Name)
**Font:** Outfit or DM Sans (sans-serif, geometric, friendly)
- **Weights:** 600 (semibold), 700 (bold)
- **Usage:** App name ("Practice Coach"), screen headers, major section titles
- **Characteristics:** Geometric and modern, but warm—not rigid. Feels like a supportive voice
- **Fallback:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif

#### Body Font (UI Text, Instructions, Copy)
**Font:** Inter or Source Sans Pro (sans-serif, highly readable, neutral)
- **Weights:** 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Usage:** All body text, buttons, labels, instructions, feedback
- **Characteristics:** Clear and accessible, warm and human—not sterile
- **Fallback:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif

#### Monospace Font (Code, Technical Labels)
**Font:** SF Mono or Roboto Mono
- **Weights:** 400, 500
- **Usage:** Note names, technical labels (if needed), time displays
- **Characteristics:** Precise, musical, matches instruction labels
- **Fallback:** monospace

## Visual Language & Style

### Design Principles

1. **Warm Approachability**
   - Human, not clinical. Like sitting with a thoughtful teacher
   - Soft, rounded corners (8px–12px standard)
   - Warm cream backgrounds (not stark white)
   - Natural, organic spacing
   - Hand-crafted but not playful (no cartoon elements)
   - Human-scale proportions

2. **Clear & Focused**
   - One question/one focus per screen
   - Visual hierarchy guides attention
   - Minimum visual noise
   - Breathing room between elements

3. **Encouraging & Supportive**
   - Warm colors convey belief in the user
   - Success moments celebrated with terracotta
   - Teal provides calm, stable anchor
   - Visual language says: "I'm here for you"

4. **Systematic Clarity**
   - Visual consistency across similar elements
   - Icons/illustrations support meaning (not decoration)
   - Logical progression feels natural, not rigid
   - Steps are clear but fluid

### Icon & Illustration Style

**Aesthetic:** Minimal line-work with warm fills. Balanced, clean geometry with organic warmth.

**Characteristics:**
- **Line weight:** 1.5–2px (not too thin, readable on mobile)
- **Stroke style:** Rounded line endings (cap: round, join: round)
- **Color:** Primarily Warm Terracotta (`#C96952`) with Warm Teal accents
- **Simplicity:** Single concept per icon, no over-complexity
- **Warmth:** Subtle fills using soft coral or cream

**Icon Categories:**

| Category | Examples | Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Navigation** | Back arrow, menu, close | Minimal line strokes |
| **Actions** | Record, play, stop, pause | Geometric, filled center |
| **Focus areas** | Pitch/bow/intonation/rhythm | Simplified instrument parts |
| **Status** | Success, warning, info | Semantic color + icon combo |
| **Instructions** | Tips, guides, hints | Lightbulb, musical notation |

**What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Cartoon/mascot style
- ❌ Photorealism
- ❌ Over-detailed line work
- ❌ Heavy drop shadows or 3D effects
- ❌ Cold, clinical aesthetics

**Icon Examples:**
- Record button: Simple circle (empty/outline) → filled circle (recording) → double circle (recorded)
- Metronome: Simple pendulum arc, not realistic
- Bow: Clean diagonal stroke, optionally with focus point
- Shift: Arrow or stylized fingerboard, minimal detail

## Layout & Grid System

### Spacing System
```
xs: 4px    (micro-spacing within components)
sm: 8px    (component padding, small gaps)
md: 16px   (standard padding, card spacing)
lg: 24px   (section spacing)
xl: 32px   (major section breaks)
```

### Mobile-First Design
- **Single column** layout on all screens
- **Full-width content** with 16px margins on mobile
- **Safe areas** respected (notch, home indicator)
- **Touch targets** minimum 44px × 44px

### Screens
- **Mobile:** 380px–480px (primary design target)
- **Tablet:** 768px–1024px (scaled up, same proportions)
- **Desktop:** 1024px+ (centered container, max 500px width to maintain intimacy)

### Negative Space Strategy
- **Top of screen:** Generous space above headers (32px minimum)
- **Between questions and inputs:** 16px–24px
- **Around buttons:** 24px–32px (allows focus without cramping)
- **Bottom of screen:** Extra breathing room before next action

## Motion & Animation

### Principles
- **Purpose-driven:** Animation clarifies, not distracts
- **Smooth & natural:** 200ms–400ms durations (not snappy, not sluggish)
- **Subtle:** Micro-interactions that feel like intuitive feedback
- **Accessible:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

### Key Animations
1. **Screen transitions:** Subtle fade in (200ms) + slight upward translation (20px)
2. **Button interactions:** 
   - Hover: Color shift + very subtle shadow (200ms)
   - Active: Immediate feedback, no delay
3. **Progress bar:** Smooth linear fill as user progresses
4. **Recording indicator:** Pulse effect on record button (timing: 1s cycle)
5. **Success moment:** Gentle scale-up + fade (400ms) for "Great work!"
6. **Hover states:** Smooth color + shadow transitions (200ms)

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Page loads with elaborate entrance animations (focus on content, not flash)
- ❌ Auto-playing animations (user action should trigger)
- ❌ Bounce or overshoot easing (feels playful, not supportive)
- ❌ Multiple simultaneous animations (overwhelming)

## Copy & Messaging Guidelines

### Vocabulary & Tone

**Use:**
- "Practice session" (not "training" or "lesson")
- "Play" / "Record yourself" (active, directional)
- "Let's work through this" (inclusive, supportive)
- "Does the problem persist?" (diagnostic, clear)
- "Give yourself a chance to settle in" (compassionate)

**Avoid:**
- "Optimize," "maximize," "achieve" (sounds corporate/clinical)
- "Repeat," "do it again" (passive, vague)
- "You should," "You must" (commanding)
- "Error," "failure," "mistake" (use "issue," "what went wrong," "the problem")
- Jargon without explanation (assume beginner-to-intermediate level)
- the em- dash "—" (it reads more like a scientific text than direct communication)

### Instruction Style
- **Short sentences** (one idea per sentence)
- **Active voice:** "Play the passage again" not "The passage should be played"
- **Specific guidance:** "Focus on the intonation of the G in bar 5" not "Fix your intonation"
- **Encouraging language:** Acknowledge effort, normalize problems, guide solutions

### Examples

**Question Phrasing:**
- ✅ "Do you want to play through or work on a short excerpt?"
- ❌ "Select practice mode"

**Instruction Phrasing:**
- ✅ "Play just the problematic section — it could be a few notes or a few bars."
- ❌ "Isolate the problem passage"

**Feedback Phrasing:**
- ✅ "Did it match your represented idea?"
- ❌ "Was it acceptable?"

## Accessibility Requirements

- **Color contrast:** WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for text)
- **Focus states:** Visible on all interactive elements (2px Deep Teal border minimum)
- **Touch targets:** 44px × 44px minimum
- **Motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable animations)
- **Text sizing:** Support 16px base font size, scalable up to 200%
- **Semantic HTML:** Proper heading hierarchy, ARIA labels where needed
- **Keyboard navigation:** All functionality accessible via keyboard
- **Mobile vibration:** Optional gentle haptic feedback on buttons (iOS)
